Use when asked to write a design doc, an RFC, a technical proposal, or an architecture decision record, or to turn a rough idea into something the team can review. # Design documents

Write `docs/rfcs/<number>-<slug>.md` with: Decision needed (top), Problem, Goals,
Non-goals, Proposal, Alternatives, Risks, Rollout, Open questions.
