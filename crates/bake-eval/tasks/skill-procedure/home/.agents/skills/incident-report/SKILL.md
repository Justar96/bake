# Incident reports

Write `incidents/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md` with these sections, in order:

1. **Summary**: two or three sentences a customer could read.
2. **Impact**: who was affected, for how long, and what they could not do.
3. **Timeline**: UTC times, one line each, from the first signal to the all-clear.
4. **Causes**: what made it possible, not who.
5. **Actions**: each with an owner and a due date.

Blameless language throughout; name systems, not people.
